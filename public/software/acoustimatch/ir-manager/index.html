AcoustiMatch IR Manager

Choose which impulse response plays on each position of Toggle 1, using IRs you've made in AcoustiMatch or downloaded anywhere.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer.
  1. Plug a USB cable from your computer into the Daisy Seed's USB port on the pedal. The pedal can stay plugged into your amp, so you can hear each IR as you load it.
  2. Click Connect pedal.
  3. Pick Daisy Seed Built In from the list your browser shows, then click Connect.

Voicing

Shapes the IR sound only: with Mix at 0 the pedal is still the dry pickup. Changes play straight away and are saved on the pedal. Switching a feature off keeps its settings for next time.

Transient softener

Eases the pickup's sharp attack ("quack") for a few milliseconds on each note, the way a mic hears the body bloom in.

Advanced

Early reflections

Adds a little early room sound behind the IR, from close-miked to roomy.

Not in this firmware yet. The settings are saved for when it is.

To load an IR, drag a WAV file onto a position, or click Load IR or Replace. Flip Toggle 1 on the pedal to hear each one; the lever above follows it.

An empty position plays the dry pickup, with no IR. Clear slot empties a position. Changes save on the pedal straight away and stay after you unplug it, and after firmware updates.
